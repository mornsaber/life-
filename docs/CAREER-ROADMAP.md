# Career Roadmap: making every career interactive

Every one of the game's 149 careers, what it has today, and what would make it as playable as academia. The academia add-on is the benchmark (`academia/`, `lab/`, `Publishing`).

## What makes academia fun

Academia works because it has all six of these:

1. **A core loop with a resource you build.** Papers, citations and grants accumulate, and every year you choose what to spend effort on.
2. **People you manage.** Lab members and PhD students have names, skills and morale. They graduate, leave or burn out.
3. **Projects with stages and odds.** Write, submit, revise, then get accepted or rejected. A grant proposal is scored and funded or not.
4. **Institutions with tiers.** Moving from a teaching college to an R1 changes everything, and you can be poached.
5. **Reputation and awards.** An h-index, named chairs, prizes: a visible score of how good you are.
6. **Meaningful yearly decisions.** Take the admin job or protect research time? Chase the risky big idea or the safe paper?

A career feels flat when it only has a ladder, a salary and occasional random events. The plan below gives each career the subset of these six that fits it, built on a handful of shared engines so 149 careers don't need 149 systems.

## Where every career stands today

| Depth | Meaning | Careers |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep** | A dedicated module with its own loop and decisions | Medicine (residency match, specialties, case files), nursing and the clinical professions (ClinicalLife), law (LegalPractice and LawLife), prosecution and public defense, police (all agencies), fire (all services), EMS (public and private), K-12 teaching, university, college and community-college faculty, research science, national labs, airline and charter pilots (logbook), transit and railroad (seniority boards), the intelligence community, the military |
| **Moments** | Ladder plus career-specific events (ProfessionMoments, WorkplaceEvents) | Most other careers: trades, finance, real estate, cosmetology, corrections, the FAA, federal law enforcement, allied health and others |
| **Ladder** | Ladder, pay and generic workplace actions only | Retail, culinary, corporate, tech, insurance, marketing, HR, design, logistics, manufacturing, childcare, libraries, public works, planning, legislative staff, regulatory agencies, the Foreign Service, the OIG, the Park Service |

## Shared engines

Seven reusable engines cover nearly every career. Each one is built once and then configured per career with names, numbers and event decks.

| Engine | Core loop | Reused by |
|---|---|---|
| **Caseload** | Named cases or clients arrive; each has stages, a difficulty and a deadline. You choose how to spend limited hours; outcomes build your record. | Social work, CPS, counseling, psychology, probation, private investigation, paralegal, public benefits claims, OIG, immigration-adjacent roles, human resources (investigations) |
| **Projects** | Staged projects with scope, budget and schedule; choose to cut corners or not; reviews, change orders, delays and awards. | Civil engineering, architecture, construction trades, game development, design, data science, cybersecurity (engagements), urban planning, DOT, public works, renewable energy, surveying |
| **Book of business** | A list of named clients or accounts with loyalty and value. Prospect, close, retain; commissions; rivals poach. | Sales, real estate, insurance, wealth/finance, cosmetology, personal training, physical therapy (private practice), veterinary, dentistry, accounting, actuarial consulting |
| **Shift & incident** | Generalises police/fire/EMS: a post or assignment, a shift pattern, a yearly volume, and decision calls with skill checks, complaints and commendations. | Dispatch, corrections and jails, private security, TSA, animal services, game wardens, park rangers, transit police, ATC, nursing units, ER-adjacent roles |
| **Season** | A year as a season: preparation choices, a variable outcome (weather, market, injuries), and a payout. | Commercial fishing, agriculture, professional sports, oil and gas (boom/bust), acting (pilot season), music (album cycle), stateFire (fire season), cruise ships (contracts) |
| **Audience** | A following that grows or shrinks with each release; virality, sponsors, burnout and controversy. | Content creation, journalism, music, acting, writing, design (portfolio), game development (launches), fitness influencers |
| **Route & logbook** | Hours, routes and qualifications logged; bid lines by seniority; checkrides and type ratings. | Trucking, airline and charter flying, flight attendants, merchant marine, railroads, transit, school buses, paratransit, logistics drivers |

## Build order

The ordering below maximises playable careers per unit of work:

1. **Shift & incident engine.** Generalise PoliceLife's call system and apply it to dispatch, corrections, security, TSA, animal services, wardens and rangers, ATC and transit police: about 10 careers.
2. **Caseload engine.** Social work, CPS, probation, counseling, psychology, PI, crime lab, claims, OIG and HR: about 10 careers.
3. **Book of business.** Sales, real estate, insurance, cosmetology, fitness, dentistry, veterinary, PT and accounting: about 9 careers.
4. **Projects engine.** Engineering, architecture, tech, game development, design, planning, DOT, data science, cybersecurity and construction: about 12 careers.
5. **Season and Audience engines.** Fishing, farming, sports, acting, music, content creation, journalism and oil and gas: about 8 careers.
6. **Career-specific large items.** Federal case investigations, Foreign Service postings, the corporate politics track, the deals pipeline, and K-12 named classes.

Each engine should follow the house patterns:
- yearly `onAgeUp` work in a module,
- prompts through `ctx.prompt` with `type: 'module.resolver'`,
- a card on the Career tab,
- rendering that never changes state,
- a test file per engine, plus additions to the click fuzzer.
